## Keep what you want to return to

Fatfish Notes keeps conversations, text, pictures and files together. Notes are saved on your device and synced through your dedicated private GitHub repository.

This handbook has three chapters. You can edit, move or delete them like any other notes. To import a fresh copy, open **Settings → Help** and choose the handbook.

## Write your first note

1. Click **New note**, enter a title, then type or paste Markdown in the body.
2. Use the toolbar for headings, lists, links, tables and code blocks. Your draft is saved locally. Use **Done** to save it as a note.
3. Choose a notebook and add a summary or tags if they will help you find it later.

> Saving and syncing happen separately. You can keep editing while offline. Saved notes sync when the connection returns.

## Find and organize your notes

Enter a word or filename, such as `Project handoff checklist.md`, in the search box. Search all notes or choose a notebook first. Use filters for tags, modification time or attachments. Click a text match to jump to the original passage.

To consolidate tags such as `handoff` and `project-handoff`, open **Organize → Tags**. Select the source tag, choose **Merge**, then look up the tag to keep. Review the affected notes before confirming. The same view previews renames and removals.

| To do this | Use this control |
| --- | --- |
| Keep important notes together | Star them and open **Favorites** |
| Keep a note near the top of its list | Choose **Pin note** in its menu |
| Change notebook order | Drag a notebook or use **Move up** and **Move down** |
| Organize several notes | Enable selection and click anywhere in each row |
| See an earlier save | Open **Version history** from the note menu |
